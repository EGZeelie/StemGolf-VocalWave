# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all contributors and community members in the StemGolf-VocalWave project, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, and sexual orientation.

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the StemGolf-VocalWave project, including but not limited to:
- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Commit messages and code reviews
- Community chat channels and forums
- Events and meetups organized under the project's name
- Any other platforms where the community interacts

## Our Standards

### Examples of Behavior That Contributes to a Positive Environment

- **Be Respectful** - Use welcoming and inclusive language. Assume good intent and interpret comments charitably.
- **Be Collaborative** - Work together toward shared goals. Credit others' work and share knowledge freely.
- **Be Constructive** - Provide helpful feedback. Critique ideas, not people. Offer solutions alongside concerns.
- **Be Patient** - Remember that this is a community of developers at different skill levels. Help newer contributors learn and grow.
- **Be Honest** - If you make a mistake, own it. If something isn't working, communicate clearly about what's needed.
- **Be Inclusive** - Welcome contributors from all backgrounds. Celebrate diverse perspectives and experiences.
- **Be Open** - Listen to feedback about your work. Be willing to change your mind when presented with new information.

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind
- Discriminatory comments or slurs based on protected characteristics
- Sexual language, advances, or unwelcome jokes of a sexual nature
- Trolling, derailing conversations, or disrupting discussions
- Publishing others' private information without consent (doxxing)
- Spam or repeated unsolicited promotional content
- Deliberately misleading or false information
- Attacking someone's character instead of addressing their ideas
- Exclusionary comments that suggest certain groups don't belong
- Excessive self-promotion without community value
- Violating copyright, intellectual property, or licensing terms

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all members of the StemGolf-VocalWave community:
- Core maintainers and project leadership
- Contributors (code, documentation, design, etc.)
- Community members (users, issue reporters, discussants)
- Anyone representing the project in a professional capacity

## Enforcement

### Reporting a Violation

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Direct Message** - Contact the project maintainers privately on GitHub
2. **Email** - Reach out to the maintainers via GitHub's private contact methods
3. **Discussion** - Use the [Discussions](https://github.com/EGZeelie/StemGolf-VocalWave/discussions) tab to flag concerns in a structured way

**Please include:**
- A clear description of the incident
- Who was involved (usernames, if comfortable sharing)
- When and where it occurred
- Any relevant context or links
- Whether you'd like the report to remain confidential

All reports will be handled with discretion. Your identity will be protected if you request confidentiality.

### Investigation & Response

Upon receiving a report, the maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** the incident thoroughly and impartially
3. **Communicate** findings and next steps to all parties involved
4. **Take Action** ranging from a private warning to temporary or permanent removal from the community

Possible actions include:
- Private warning or education
- Temporary suspension from community interactions
- Permanent removal from the project and blocking
- Escalation to GitHub Trust & Safety team

The severity and context of the violation will determine the appropriate response.

### Appeal Process

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may submit an appeal within 30 days of the action, detailing:
- The original incident and action taken
- Why you believe the decision was incorrect
- What changes have occurred (if applicable)

Appeals will be reviewed by project leadership.

## Community Responsibilities

### For Contributors

- Follow the Code of Conduct in all interactions
- Review the [Contributing Guidelines](./CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting code
- Write clear, respectful commit messages
- Be receptive to feedback on your work
- Help mentor newer contributors
- Report violations you become aware of

### For Maintainers

- Model exemplary conduct in all interactions
- Enforce the Code of Conduct consistently and fairly
- Provide constructive feedback rather than dismissive criticism
- Acknowledge and appreciate contributions
- Make decisions transparently when possible
- Create a safe environment for reporting and discussion

### For All Community Members

- Be an active upstander—speak up against inappropriate behavior
- Welcome new members with kindness
- Share knowledge and help others learn
- Respect licensing, copyright, and intellectual property
- Give credit where it's due
- Assume good intent, but address impact

## Anti-Harassment Policy

We do not tolerate harassment in any form. Harassment includes:

- Unwelcome comments regarding someone's identity (race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, etc.)
- Threats or intimidation
- Deliberate misgendering or use of incorrect pronouns
- Sexual harassment of any kind
- Unwanted contact that continues after being asked to stop
- Disruption of discussions or events
- Posting private information without consent

If you experience harassment, please report it immediately using the channels outlined above.

## Diversity & Inclusion

StemGolf-VocalWave thrives through diverse perspectives. We actively encourage contributions from:

- Developers of all experience levels
- Musicians, producers, and audio engineers
- People from underrepresented groups in tech
- Non-native English speakers (we'll help with clarity if needed)
- Contributors in different time zones and regions

We recognize that audio production and software development attract people from varied backgrounds. Our community is stronger when we listen to and learn from each other.
